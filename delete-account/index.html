刪除你的 Yellow Grapes 帳號

你可以隨時刪除 Yellow Grapes 帳號。刪除後，你的帳號與個人資料會被永久移除，無法復原。

在 App 中刪除（最快）

  1. 打開 Yellow Grapes，點地圖上的個人頭像，進入個人頁面。
  2. 點「進階設定」。
  3. 在「帳號」下方點「刪除帳號」，並確認。

為了保護你的帳號，App 可能會請你先重新登入一次。確認後會立即刪除。

如果你是商家的店主，請先到該商家的「店內夥伴」頁面轉讓或放棄商家管理權，才能刪除帳號。

無法使用 App 時

請用帳號登入時使用的電子郵件寄信至 contact@grapes.com.tw， 主旨寫「刪除帳號」，並附上你的帳號 ID（如果記得）。我們確認帳號屬於你之後，會在 30 天內完成刪除並回信通知。

會被刪除的資料

  • 登入帳號，以及個人資料：名稱、帳號 ID、出生年份、頭像、電子郵件
  • 收藏的店家
  • 「我的葡萄酒」中的打卡、筆記與照片
  • 你寫的評論與評分
  • 支持店家、活動的「有興趣」
  • 通知與搜尋紀錄
  • 認領商家的申請、店內夥伴身分與邀請
  • 貴賓款待計畫的會員代碼、使用紀錄與訂閱資料

你上傳到店家酒單的葡萄酒會被撤下，酒款資料一併清除。

會保留、但不再與你連結的資料

你提出的店家資料修改建議、新店家建議、檢舉與問題回報的文字內容，以及相關的管理紀錄，會保留作為審核紀錄，但改標示為「已刪除的使用者」，不再與你的帳號或身分連結。問題回報附上的截圖會被刪除。

保存期限

  • 備份：系統的加密備份依排程自動汰換（每日備份保留 7 天、每週備份 28 天、每月備份 12 個月）。已刪除的資料最遲會在 12 個月內從所有備份中消失。
  • 使用統計與當機報告：不含你的名稱或電子郵件，由 Google Analytics 最多保存 14 個月、Firebase Crashlytics 保存 90 天後自動刪除。
  • 依法須保存的紀錄（例如付款發票，如有）：依法律規定的期限保存。

完整說明請見隱私權政策。

Delete your Yellow Grapes account

You can delete your Yellow Grapes account at any time. Your account and personal data are then removed permanently. This can't be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Yellow Grapes and tap your profile picture on the map to open your profile.
  2. Tap Advanced settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

To protect your account, the app may ask you to sign in again first. Deletion happens immediately once you confirm.

If you own a business on Yellow Grapes, transfer or release ownership on that business's team page first. The app won't delete an account that still owns a business.

If you can't use the app

Email contact@grapes.com.tw from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my account”, and include your Account ID if you know it. Once we've confirmed the account is yours, we'll delete it within 30 days and reply to let you know.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile: name, Account ID, birth year, profile picture and email address
  • Your saved venues
  • Your wine check-ins, notes and photos in My Wines
  • Your reviews and star ratings
  • Your venue support votes and event interest
  • Your notifications and search history
  • Your business claim requests, team memberships and invitations
  • Your Member Benefits Program member codes, redemption history and subscription records

Wines you uploaded to venues' wine lists are withdrawn and their details erased.

What is kept, no longer linked to you

Edit suggestions, new-venue suggestions, reports and the text of app feedback you sent, along with related moderation logs, are kept as a review record. They are relabelled “deleted user” and are no longer linked to your account or identity. Screenshots attached to app feedback are deleted.

Retention periods

  • Backups: encrypted system backups are replaced on a schedule (daily backups kept 7 days, weekly 28 days, monthly 12 months). Deleted data is gone from every backup within 12 months.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports: these don't include your name or email. Google Analytics keeps them for at most 14 months and Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, then deletes them automatically.
  • Records the law requires us to keep (such as payment invoices, if any): kept for the period the law requires.

For details, see our Privacy Policy.
