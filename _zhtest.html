Taiwanese palates are remarkably discerning.

Give us the same plate of chicken rice and everyone knows exactly which street corner serves it at its most delicious. Faced with a street full of bubble-tea stands, we can still taste which one to buy our green tea from, whose milk is the best, and which one slipped in a harsh, artificial fruit syrup.

The beauty of wine hides in the most delicate registers of taste and smell — and it takes exactly this kind of keen sense to feel and savour the interplay and shifting of flavour and aroma on the palate.

We built this wine map for everyone in Taiwan who loves wine — so that together we can set out on a more refined journey for the palate.
